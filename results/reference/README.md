# Reference results of the revised manuscript

Generated by `analysis_revision/make_reference_readme.py` from `facts.json` (same folder). Do not edit
by hand: regenerate. `facts.json` is written by `analysis_revision/build_revision.py` from the raw
simulation outputs, with the same code that writes the tables of the manuscript, so these tables and
those of the manuscript cannot differ.

## What the values are

- Means and 95% confidence intervals of the 3900 simulations behind the tables of the
  revised manuscript (2730 for the seven techniques of the per-workload tables).
- PlanetLab: ten daily traces × 30 seeds per technique; the confidence interval is computed over the
  ten daily means, because runs of the same day share the trace. Alibaba, Materna and Microsoft Azure:
  one trace × 30 seeds; the interval is computed over the 30 seeds.
- The tables of the three experiments added by the revision (closed-loop replacement of the forecaster,
  sensitivity to the Bollinger parameters, forecast accuracy) are in `experiments/revision_r1/tables/`.

## Definitions

- **Host energy**: energy of the active hosts plus host start-up and shutdown.
- **Migration energy**: sum over the migrations of `kappa × hops × MB + beta`, with kappa = 0.512 J per MB
  and hop and beta = 20.165 J per migration. `MB` is the memory of the migrated VM and `hops` the number of
  links of the shortest path between source and destination hosts. The two coefficients are those of the model
  of Liu et al. (HPDC 2011, Eq. 17: E = 0.512·V + 20.165, with V in megabytes and E in joules). No coefficient
  was measured by the authors.
- **Total energy**: host energy plus migration energy.
- **Migration traffic**: memory moved by the migrations weighted by the hops of each path, in GB·hop. Each
  migration is counted once. The simulator registers every migration twice in its network accounting
  (`totalRAMInBW`), so the scripts halve that field.
- **Mean active hosts**: active hosts averaged over the 288 samples of a simulated day.
- **ME ratio**: executed MIPS per kWh of total energy (higher is better).
- **Inter-VM link load**: bandwidth occupied by the communications between VMs, accumulated over links and
  samples (lower means communicating VMs are placed closer).

The tables of the submitted version (24 September 2026) used a different energy expression: migration traffic
as registered by the simulator (every migration counted twice), 4.096 J per MB and a 1.1 factor. 4.096 is eight
times the coefficient of the model; it is not a measurement. Files that still carry those values are marked as
such.

## Comparing a re-execution with these tables

Compare aggregates (mean and confidence interval per technique and workload), not individual runs.

- Individual runs are not reproducible bit for bit, for any of the techniques. At several points of a decision
  the implementation chooses among equivalent candidates (hosts of equal capacity, VMs with the same heuristic
  value, the order in which overloaded hosts are processed), and it resolves those ties by the iteration order
  of hash-based collections, which depends on the state of the Java runtime and not only on the seed. The
  results are therefore distributions over seeds and traces.
- The runs of WPSP, WP and WBP were executed again (13 traces × 3 techniques × 30 seeds = 1,170 runs, one
  simulation per JVM) and the means of the two campaigns agree: one of the 60 paired comparisons (three
  techniques, four workloads, five metrics) has p < 0.05 before correction for multiple tests. The comparison is
  in `experiments/revision_r1/tables/rerun_vs_stored.md`.
- The runs of the other techniques have not been executed again.

## Statistical tests

`stats.md` (same folder) lists the 360 paired tests of WBP against each of the other nine techniques on
the ten metrics and the four workloads, with raw and Holm-adjusted p-values. PlanetLab: paired t-test on the ten
daily means. Other workloads: Wilcoxon signed-rank test on the 30 seed-paired runs.

## How to regenerate

See `analysis_revision/README.md`: extract the per-run cache from `output/`, run `build_revision.py`, copy
`facts.json` and `stats.md` here and run this script.
