# Tables of the experiments of the revision: index

All tables are generated by the scripts in `../tools/` and `../forecast_accuracy/`; none is edited by
hand. The `.tex` files are the tables included in the revised manuscript.

| File | Table of the manuscript | Content | Runs behind it |
|---|---|---|---|
| `closed_loop.md`, `tab_closed_loop.tex` | `tab:closed_loop` | six closed-loop variants (LVP, LVP+BB, EWMA, EWMA+BB, Prophet, Prophet+BB), 13 traces × 30 seeds, with paired contrasts and decision time without contention | 2,340 + 48 timing runs |
| `rerun_vs_stored.md` | none (figures quoted in the text on the reproducibility of a run) | WPSP, WP and WBP of the closed-loop campaign (LVP, Prophet, Prophet+BB) against the campaign behind the main tables, same traces and seeds | 1,170 of the 2,340 runs |
| `bollinger_sensitivity.md`, `tab_bollinger_sensitivity.tex` | `tab:bollinger_sensitivity` | WBP with N in 3, 5, 10, 20 (alpha 0.5) and alpha in 0.5, 1.0, 1.5, 2.0 (N 5), 13 traces × seeds 0-9, with paired contrasts against (5, 0.5) | 780 + the 130 runs of the reference setting |
| `forecast_accuracy.md`, `tab_forecast_accuracy.tex` | `tab:forecast_val_u` | forecast accuracy on the series the simulator reads: protocol A, protocol B and protocol B with the Bollinger input | no simulation; 1,700 VM blocks |
| `inventory.md` | none | complete runs per family, workload and variant | 3,168 |

Folder prefixes of the raw outputs: `cl_` closed loop, `bs_` Bollinger sensitivity, `tm_` decision time.

How to rebuild them: see `../README.md`.
